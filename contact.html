Helpful Information

Frequently Asked Questions

Key details to help you plan your dining experience or catering booking.

How far in advance should I book catering?

For weddings and large corporate events (100+ guests), we recommend booking 3 to 8 weeks in advance to secure your date and schedule a menu tasting. Smaller private dinners or family gatherings require at least 3 to 5 business days' notice.

Do you cater outside of Nairobi?

Yes! Chef Mwikali and her mobile catering brigade travel across Kiambu, Naivasha, Nakuru, Machakos, and the Kenyan Coast. Out-of-station logistics and transport are clearly quoted in your customised proposal.

Are your ingredients Halal and dietary compliant?

Yes. All beef, goat, and poultry are 100% sourced from certified Halal butcheries in Kenya. We also offer dedicated vegan, gluten-free, and nut-allergy compliant menus prepared with separate cookware.

How does online food ordering work?

Select dishes from our digital menu and submit your order enquiry. You will receive an instant order reference and a pre-filled WhatsApp link. Once Chef Mwikali confirms kitchen availability, payment arrangements are finalized.
