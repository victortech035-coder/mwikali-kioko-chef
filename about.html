Meet the Chef

Culinary Passion, Heritage & Craft

Discovering the story behind Chef Mwikali Kioko and her vision for contemporary Kenyan hospitality.

Her Story & Philosophy

"Food is the Heart of Every Celebration"

Chef Mwikali Kioko grew up surrounded by the rich aromas of Kenyan family kitchens — slow-roasted meats, bubbling pots of spiced pilau, and farm-fresh greens picked at dawn.

Her culinary philosophy is simple: honour authentic traditional flavours while applying modern culinary discipline, exquisite plating, and uncompromised food safety. Every dish should not only satisfy hunger, but spark conversation and fond memories.

From high-energy wedding receptions in Karen and Lake Naivasha to intimate private dinners in Runda and Westlands, Chef Mwikali leads a kitchen brigade that values precision, warmth, and genuine care for guests.

Expertise

Areas of Specialisation

Versatile culinary styles tailored to diverse event occasions and guest palates.

Swahili & Coastal Cuisine

Mastery of traditional coastal spicing — slow-steamed beef and chicken pilau, coconut-curried kuku paka, freshly pressed tamarind marinades, and hand-rolled mahamri.

Traditional Kenyan Grilling

Live open-flame charcoal grilling (nyama choma), prime goat and beef ribs seasoned with rosemary-infused Kenyan sea salt, complemented by authentic mukimo and kachumbari.

Modern Banquet Presentation

Polished multi-tier buffet stations, elegant canapé platters for corporate cocktails, plated multi-course fine dining, and bespoke dietary adaptations (Halal, Vegan, Gluten-Free).

Our Promise

Uncompromising Quality, Hygiene & Elegance

In Chef Mwikali's kitchen, food safety and ingredient integrity are never compromised. We source only from certified local butcheries, organic vegetable suppliers, and trusted spice merchants. All staff follow rigorous sanitisation guidelines, wear professional executive kitchen attire, and handle service with the utmost respect for your guests.
